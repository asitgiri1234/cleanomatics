# Subscription Plans and Cancellation

*ShipFlow policy documentation. This covers the ShipFlow subscription itself —
the plan a business pays for to use the platform.*

## Plans

| Plan | Price (monthly) | Orders / month | Users | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | $29 | up to 500 | 1 | Standard |
| Growth | $89 | up to 5,000 | 5 | Priority |
| Scale | $249 | up to 25,000 | 20 | Priority + phone |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Unlimited | Custom | Dedicated manager |

Growth adds the Returns Center. Scale adds API access, custom carrier rates,
and a 99.9% uptime SLA.

## Annual billing

Paying annually costs the equivalent of **10 months instead of 12** — roughly
17% off. Annual plans are charged as a single upfront payment.

## Free trial

Starter and Growth include a **14-day free trial**. No credit card is required
to start it. If no plan is chosen when the trial ends, the account becomes
read-only rather than being charged.

## Order overages

Orders above the plan limit are billed at **$0.03 per order** and appear on the
next invoice. Overages never interrupt service — orders keep syncing.

## Changing plan

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately, and the remainder of the current period
  is charged pro rata.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the next renewal date, so the current plan runs
  to the end of the period already paid for.

## Cancellation

A plan can be cancelled at any time from **Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan**.
Only the account Owner can cancel.

- Access continues to the **end of the current billing period**. There is no
  immediate cut-off.
- Monthly plans are **not refunded** for the unused part of the month.
- Annual plans cancelled within **30 days of the first purchase** are fully
  refunded. After 30 days, an annual plan runs to the end of its term.

## After cancellation

- The account stays **read-only for 60 days**, so data can still be viewed and
  exported.
- Reactivating within **90 days** restores everything.
- Data is permanently deleted **90 days** after cancellation.
