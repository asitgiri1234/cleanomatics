# ShipFlow General FAQ

*ShipFlow policy documentation. General questions about the product, support,
and security.*

## What is ShipFlow?

ShipFlow is a SaaS platform for online businesses. It brings orders, shipping
and tracking, returns and refunds, subscriptions and billing, and customer
accounts into one dashboard, so a merchant does not have to run those in
separate tools.

## Who is it for?

Online retailers shipping anywhere from a few dozen to tens of thousands of
orders a month. The plan tiers are sized around that range.

## What does it connect to?

ShipFlow syncs orders from Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and Magento.
Direct API access, for a custom or in-house storefront, is included on the Scale
plan and above.

## How long does setup take?

Connecting a store and importing existing orders usually takes under an hour.
Historical orders from the previous 12 months are imported automatically.

## Is there a mobile app?

No. ShipFlow is a web application. It works in a mobile browser, but there is no
native iOS or Android app.

## How do I contact support?

| Channel | Available on | Response target |
|---|---|---|
| Email | All plans | 1 business day |
| Live chat | Growth and above | 4 business hours |
| Phone | Scale and above | Same business day |

Support hours are **Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM Eastern**, excluding US
public holidays.

## Is my data secure?

ShipFlow is SOC 2 Type II certified. Data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.2
or higher and encrypted at rest. Card details are handled by a PCI DSS Level 1
payment processor and are never stored on ShipFlow servers. Data is hosted in
the United States.

## Is there an uptime guarantee?

A 99.9% uptime SLA is included on Scale and Enterprise plans. Current and
historical uptime is published on the ShipFlow status page.

## What languages are supported?

The dashboard and support are available in English only.

## Where do I find answers about a specific order?

Individual order details — where a package is, what a customer was charged, what
stage a return is at — are not in this documentation. They are looked up live
from the order record. These documents cover policy: how long delivery takes,
what the return window is, how refunds are calculated.
