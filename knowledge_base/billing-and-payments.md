# Billing and Payments

*ShipFlow policy documentation. This covers how ShipFlow charges for the
platform subscription. It does not contain any account's invoice history.*

## Billing cycle

Subscriptions renew automatically on the **anniversary of the sign-up date** —
monthly or annually, depending on the plan chosen. A renewal reminder is emailed
**7 days before** an annual charge.

## Accepted payment methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- ACH bank debit — US accounts on **annual** plans only

Cheques, wire transfers, cryptocurrency, and PayPal are not accepted. All
charges are in **US dollars**; ShipFlow does not bill in other currencies.

## Invoices and receipts

Every charge produces an invoice, emailed to the billing contact and available
as a PDF under **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. The billing contact can be a
different person from the account Owner. Invoices show the plan charge, any
order overages, and tax as separate lines.

## Tax

Sales tax or VAT is added based on the billing address. Businesses in the EU
and UK can add a VAT ID under **Settings → Billing → Tax details** to have VAT
reverse-charged.

## Failed payments

When a charge fails, the card is retried on **days 3, 5, and 7**. A payment
failure notice goes to both the Owner and the billing contact each time.

| Days past due | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1–6 | Full access, retries continue |
| 7 | Account becomes read-only |
| 14 | Account suspended, order syncing stops |
| 30 | Subscription cancelled |

Paying the outstanding invoice at any point before day 30 restores access
immediately.

## Overage charges

Orders processed above the plan limit are billed at **$0.03 per order** on the
following invoice. Overages are never charged mid-cycle.

## Subscription refunds

Subscription charges follow the rules in the subscription document: annual
plans are refundable within 30 days of the first purchase, monthly plans are
not refunded for unused time. This is separate from refunds on customer orders.
