# Refund Policy

*ShipFlow policy documentation. This covers refunds on customer orders. Refunds
of ShipFlow subscription charges are covered in the subscription document. This
document does not contain the status of any individual refund.*

## When a refund is issued

| Situation | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Order cancelled before the label is created | Full refund, no fees |
| Returned item passes inspection | Refund minus return shipping |
| Item faulty, damaged, or incorrect | Full refund including all shipping |
| Package confirmed lost in transit | Full refund or replacement, customer's choice |
| Item used or missing packaging | Partial refund, up to 20% deducted |

## Timing

Refunds are approved within **3–5 business days** of the return passing
inspection, or immediately for a cancellation before shipment. After approval,
the bank or card issuer can take up to a further **10 business days** to post
the money. Total worst case is around two to three weeks from the item arriving
back.

## Shipping costs

- The **$6.95 return shipping fee** is deducted from the refund on a standard
  return.
- The **original outbound shipping charge** is refunded only when the whole
  order is returned, or when the item was faulty, damaged, or incorrect.
- On a partial return, outbound shipping is not refunded.

## Refund method

Refunds go back to the original payment method. If that card has expired or the
account is closed, the refund is issued as store credit or, on request, by bank
transfer. Refunds cannot be redirected to a different card.

## Store credit

Store credit is offered as an alternative to a cash refund. It is issued
**immediately** on approval rather than after the 3–5 day window, the $6.95
return shipping fee is not deducted, and it does not expire.

## Late or missing refunds

If the refund has been approved and more than 10 business days have passed
since approval, contact support with the order reference. Refunds that have not
yet reached approval are still inside the normal window.
