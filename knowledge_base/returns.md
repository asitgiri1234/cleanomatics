# Returns

*ShipFlow policy documentation. This describes the return process. It does not
contain the status of any individual order or return.*

## Return window

Items can be returned within **30 days of the delivery date**. The window is
measured from delivery, not from the order date. Requests made after 30 days
are declined automatically.

## Item condition

To be accepted, a returned item must be:

- unused and undamaged,
- in its original packaging,
- with tags and accessories still attached.

Items that arrive showing light use or missing packaging may be accepted with a
partial refund of up to **20% deducted**. Items that arrive damaged by the
customer are declined and can be shipped back on request.

## Items that cannot be returned

- Perishable goods
- Personalised or custom-made items
- Digital downloads
- Gift cards
- Opened hygiene, beauty, or personal-care products

Faulty items in these categories are still covered — report them instead of
opening a standard return.

## How a return works

1. Open the **Returns Center** and select the order and items.
2. The request is reviewed and approved or declined within **1 business day**.
3. A prepaid return label is emailed on approval.
4. The item must be shipped back within **14 days** of approval, or the return
   is cancelled.
5. Once the item arrives, inspection is completed within **3 business days**.
6. The refund is issued after inspection passes.

## Return shipping cost

Return shipping is a flat **$6.95**, deducted from the refund. It is **waived**
when the item was damaged in transit, faulty, or not what was ordered — in
those cases the return is free.

## Exchanges

There is no direct exchange flow. An exchange is handled as a return plus a new
order, so the replacement can be placed immediately rather than waiting for the
original item to come back.
