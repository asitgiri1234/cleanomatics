# Shipping and Delivery

*ShipFlow policy documentation. This describes how shipping works on the
platform. It does not contain the status of any individual order.*

## Service levels and transit times

Transit time is counted in business days from the day the order ships, not
from the day it was placed.

| Service | Transit time | Tracking |
|---|---|---|
| Economy | 5–8 business days | Yes |
| Standard | 3–5 business days | Yes |
| Express | 2 business days | Yes |
| Overnight | Next business day | Yes |

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding US public holidays. Carriers do
not pick up or deliver on weekends or holidays.

## Processing and cutoff times

Orders confirmed before **2:00 PM local warehouse time** are picked, packed and
handed to the carrier the same business day. Orders confirmed after the cutoff
ship the next business day. Most orders spend 1 business day in processing
before the label is created.

## Tracking

A tracking number is generated when the shipping label is created, and the
order moves to Shipped. The first carrier scan usually appears within 24 hours
of label creation — until then the tracking number is valid but shows no
movement, which is normal.

Tracking updates are pulled from the carrier roughly every 4 hours.

## Shipping costs

- Standard shipping is **free on orders of $75 or more**.
- Below $75, Standard shipping is a flat **$5.95**.
- Express is **$14.95**, Overnight is **$29.95**, Economy is **$3.95**.
- Shipping charges are calculated at checkout and shown before payment.

## International shipping

ShipFlow ships to 40 countries. International delivery takes **7–21 business
days**. Time spent in customs is outside carrier control and is not included in
that estimate. Duties and import taxes are the recipient's responsibility and
are collected by the carrier or customs authority on delivery, not by ShipFlow.

## Split shipments and backorders

If part of an order is unavailable, the in-stock items ship first and the rest
follow when stock arrives. Each shipment gets its own tracking number. There is
no extra shipping charge for a split shipment.

## Address changes

A delivery address can be changed only **before the shipping label is
created**. Once the label exists the address is locked and the package must be
delivered, refused, or returned before it can be re-sent.

## Delayed and lost packages

A package is treated as potentially lost when tracking shows no movement for
**7 business days** (domestic) or **21 business days** (international). At that
point a carrier investigation can be opened, and investigations close within
**8 business days**. If the package is confirmed lost, the customer may choose
a replacement or a full refund.

Claims must be opened within **30 days of the ship date**.
